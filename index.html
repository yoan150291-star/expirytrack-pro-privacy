ExpiryTrack Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Provider: Vikram Shakti Bablee, developer of ExpiryTrack Pro

Contact: expirytrackerpro@gmail.com

This policy explains how ExpiryTrack Pro handles information when you use its Android application. ExpiryTrack Pro helps you record stock counts, expiry batches, supermarket Rotation checks and shelf-refill lists.

Information you enter

Company and supermarket names, product details and barcodes, quantities, dates, saved drafts, completed records and settings are stored in the app's local database on your device. Optional Rotation verification stores the reviewer's entered name, role, signature drawing and review time. Obtain the person's permission before recording or sharing their verification information. The app does not authenticate a reviewer's professional authority.

The current version does not provide an app sign-in account or synchronise your working database to an ExpiryTrack Pro cloud workspace. Removing a company from the active catalogue is not permanent deletion of its historical records.

Barcode scanning and camera permission

Camera permission is requested for barcode scanning. You may deny or revoke it and enter products manually. Camera frames are processed on the device using Google's bundled ML Kit barcode scanner. The app's scanner does not save photographs or upload camera frames, and ML Kit does not send its input images or recognition output to Google for processing.

ML Kit does communicate with Google for service maintenance and diagnostic/usage metrics. Google's disclosures describe device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance and configuration information, feature events and error codes. This is distinct from sending the barcode camera image. See ML Kit privacy information, ML Kit Android data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy.

Public product lookup

In the current closed-test build (version 49), external product lookup is disabled. Scanned barcodes are matched against your local catalogues. If no local match is found, you can enter the product manually. This build does not send barcode lookup requests to Open Food Facts. Existing local product records are retained. The external lookup implementation is reserved for a future reviewed release; any change to these data practices will be disclosed before it is enabled.

Catalogue imports and report sharing

When you select a catalogue document, the app reads that document to import product information. It does not require general access to your contacts, location or entire media library.

Report images are generated in the app's cache. When you share them, Android provides the chosen receiving app access to those images. A report may include company/store information, quantities, dates and reviewer/signature details. Check the report and recipient before sharing. The receiving service's privacy practices apply, and clearing local data does not recall copies already sent to other people or services. Cached report images can remain until the app's cache is cleared by you or Android.

Google Play subscriptions

Subscription prices, offers and existing purchases are queried through Google Play Billing. Google Play manages its payment process and account information; ExpiryTrack Pro does not ask you to enter a payment card in the app or receive your full card details. Google's applicable privacy and payment terms govern Google's processing.

The current release-preparation version has Subscribe disabled while secure purchase verification is being configured. It does not currently send purchase tokens to an ExpiryTrack Pro verification server. Before an online verification service or app authentication account is enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the service provider, identifiers and purchase information processed, purposes, retention and deletion arrangements. Subscription expiry must not delete your local working records.

Backup, retention and deletion

Android backup and device transfer are currently enabled for the app. Depending on your device, Google account and backup settings, the operating system may back up or transfer the database and settings. This is an Android service, not an ExpiryTrack Pro workspace-sync feature. Review your device's backup controls and the provider's policy.

Local information remains until removed by available app controls, app-data clearing, uninstalling or operating-system actions. Verified Rotation reports are protected from ordinary editing and deletion inside the app. Company removal is a soft removal, not a promise of erasure. Clearing all app data or uninstalling can permanently remove local work; external report copies and system backups may remain under their own controls. Do not uninstall to install an ordinary update without first safeguarding your data. Uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

We cannot remotely erase a database that exists only on your device. For assistance, contact expirytrackerpro@gmail.com. Requests relating to Google backup, Google Play or a report recipient may also need to be addressed to that provider. If you email support, the support mailbox receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Avoid sending payment secrets, private signatures or full customer catalogues unnecessarily. Email is handled outside the app; clearing app data does not delete sent emails or copies in a mailbox. Contact the support address with questions or requests concerning that correspondence.

Security and your choices

Protect your device with a screen lock and share operational information only with appropriate recipients. Lookup requests use HTTPS and report access uses Android's sharing permissions. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

You control camera permission, selected import files, entered data, report recipients and device backup settings. Manual entry is available without requiring a public product match. The app is intended for adult professional work rather than children.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts or sign-in form added by ExpiryTrack Pro. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security when serving GitHub Pages websites. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. Visiting this page does not upload your app database or reports.
